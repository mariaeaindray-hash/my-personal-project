MY SCHOOL

Studying Japanese in Nagasaki

I came to Nagasaki to study Japanese at Metro IT Business College. My main purpose for coming to the school was to study Japanese and improve my Japanese language skills.

During my time at the school, I studied Japanese grammar, vocabulary, reading, writing, listening, and conversation. Studying Japanese every day helped me become more familiar with the language and gave me more confidence in using it.

Living in Japan while studying Japanese was also an important part of my experience. I could practice Japanese not only in the classroom, but also in everyday situations.

LIFE IN NAGASAKI

My Life Outside School

My life in Nagasaki was not only about studying. I also enjoyed exploring the city and discovering new places.

I liked walking around Nagasaki, visiting beautiful places, taking photos, and trying different kinds of food. These experiences helped me learn more about Japanese culture and everyday life.

Nagasaki has a unique atmosphere, with its history, beautiful views, streets, and nearby sea. Living here gave me many memories that I would like to keep and share.
