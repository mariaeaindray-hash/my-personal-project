MY FAVORITES

Not everything has to be a Nagasaki specialty ✿

Nagasaki has many famous local foods, but the restaurants I want to share here are a little different. They are not all Nagasaki specialties. They are simply places I enjoyed visiting and eating at during my time here.

Some became places I visited with friends, some gave me beautiful memories, and some are places I would happily visit again.

MY FAVORITE VIEW

Attic

📍 Dejima Wharf, Nagasaki

Attic is one of the restaurants I enjoyed visiting because of its beautiful location beside Nagasaki Port. The view makes the whole experience feel special.

I ordered dessert, wine, and pizza. For me, the food itself was just okay, but the atmosphere and the view were worth it. I would definitely recommend this place if you want to relax, enjoy the harbor scenery, and spend time with friends.

ONE OF MY FAVORITES

Ramen Hiiragi

📍 Dozamachi, Nagasaki

Ramen Hiiragi is one of the best ramen shops I have ever been to. It is very popular, so you may have to wait in line, but I think it is worth it.

My favorite is the tantanmen. I like it so much that I always want to go back whenever I have the chance. This is one of the restaurants I would recommend most strongly to someone who loves ramen.

WITH FRIENDS

KIKI-MAIMAI

📍 Hamamachi, Nagasaki

KIKI-MAIMAI is a Vietnamese restaurant that my friends and I enjoyed visiting together. It became one of our favorite places when we wanted something different.

The pho is especially delicious. I really enjoy the fresh and comforting taste, and it is a place that brings back good memories of spending time with my friends.

SWEET BREAK

Gelato in Hamanomachi

📍 Hamanomachi Shopping Street

I do not remember the exact name of this gelato shop, but I remember how creamy and delicious the ice cream was. I found it inside the famous Hamanomachi shopping area.

It was the kind of dessert that was perfect for a little break while walking around the shopping street. I especially liked the creamy texture and sweet taste.

THAI FOOD

かうけん

📍 Furumachi, Nagasaki

かうけん is a Thai restaurant that I really enjoyed. It is a small restaurant, but the food has a lot of flavor and feels very comforting.

My recommendations are the kapung-style fried pork dish and the pad thai. I especially like the crispy fried pork and the noodles, and I think this place is worth trying if you enjoy Thai food.

HIGHLY RECOMMENDED

Hamakatsu

📍 Nagasaki

Hamakatsu is part of the same company group as Ringer Hut, the restaurant chain known for champon. Hamakatsu specializes in tonkatsu and other fried dishes.

This is one of the restaurants I highly recommend trying when you visit Nagasaki. I really enjoy the crispy fried meat, and it is a great choice when you want a filling and satisfying Japanese meal.
