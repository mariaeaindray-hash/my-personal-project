MY TOP FIVE

Nagasaki has many beautiful places ✿

There are many interesting places to visit in Nagasaki, such as Dejima, Hamanomachi, and many other beautiful areas around the city. But from all the places I have visited, these five are my personal favorites.

Each place gave me a different memory, from enjoying a beautiful night view to relaxing by the sea and exploring the unique atmosphere of Nagasaki.

01

Inasayama Night View

📍 Fuchi-machi, Nagasaki City

Inasayama is one of my favorite places in Nagasaki, especially at night. From the mountain, you can see the city lights spread across Nagasaki and the harbor. The view is beautiful and creates a very calm and romantic atmosphere.

I think it is a wonderful place to visit in the evening, especially for people who enjoy photography and beautiful city views.

02

Minato Park

📍 Shinchi, Nagasaki City

Minato Park is a perfect place to walk and relax. It is located next to Nagasaki Shinchi Chinatown, so it is easy to visit both places together.

I like the peaceful atmosphere of the park. It is a nice place to take a break, enjoy the surroundings, and spend some quiet time away from the busier streets.

03

Nagasaki Shinchi Chinatown

📍 Shinchi-machi, Nagasaki City

Nagasaki Shinchi Chinatown is one of the most famous areas in the city. I really like its colorful streets, gates, restaurants, and unique atmosphere.

It is also a great place to enjoy food. You can find many Chinese restaurants, local Nagasaki dishes, sweets, and other interesting foods while walking around the area.

04

Iojima Island Beach

📍 Iojima, Nagasaki City

Iojima Island Beach is one of the most memorable places I visited during my time in Nagasaki.

I went there when my school organized a school trip. Spending time with my classmates by the beach made the experience especially enjoyable and memorable.

I loved the blue sea, the open sky, and the relaxing atmosphere. It was a nice break from everyday school life and one of the memories I will remember from studying in Nagasaki.

05

Glover Garden

📍 Minami-Yamate, Nagasaki City

Glover Garden is a beautiful historical area on a hill overlooking Nagasaki Port. I like the old Western-style buildings, stone paths, flowers, and the view of the city.

It is a great place for walking and taking photos. The combination of history, architecture, gardens, and scenery gives the place a special atmosphere.
